Fossicking

A creek in the gem and gold country. Your claim, your shovel, your luck.

  1. Sweep the detector for nuggets. High tone: gold (or brass). Low growl: iron junk. Gems don't beep.
  2. Dig with the shovel. Every shovelful goes in your bucket as wash. The good stuff sits deep, right on bedrock.
  3. In the creek, pan wash for gold, or wet-sieve it for sapphires, zircons and agates.
  4. Keep an eye out for agates and glints lying on gravel bars and slopes. Press E to pick them up.
  5. Read the water and the hills: press N for your field notes. Sell at the Gold & Gems table at camp.
